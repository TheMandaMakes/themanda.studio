Welcome to TheManda—my little corner of the internet!

This is a place where I explore, express, and make things. You'll find websites I'm building, front-end experiments, ideas that won't leave me alone, creative projects, photographs, and reflections from life along the way.
At its heart, this isn't about having all the answers or showcasing polished work. It's about paying attention. It's where I process life through creativity, document what I'm learning, and leave little pieces of myself along the way. Every project, photograph, journal entry, and line of code is part of the same story—one of growth, curiosity, and becoming.
Thanks for being here. I hope you find something that inspires you to make, create, or simply notice the beauty around you.
